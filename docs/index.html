Questions.

Is this the same as Apple's Dual Capture?

It's the same idea, recording the front and back cameras at once, built independently with Apple's public camera APIs. DualShot also saves each camera as its own file and takes dual photos.

Why doesn't my older iPhone have this already?

Apple offers Dual Capture only in the Camera app on the iPhone 17 lineup. The ability to run two cameras at once has been available to apps since iOS 13 on iPhones with an A12 chip or newer.

Will it overheat my iPhone?

Running two cameras is demanding. With Heat-Up Settings on, DualShot lowers the frame rate as your iPhone warms up so recording keeps going. You can turn it off if you'd rather keep full quality.

Does it record sound?

Yes, one microphone track shared by both files, so they stay in sync.

How do I get it?
