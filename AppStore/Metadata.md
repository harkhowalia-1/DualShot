**Description (4000)**
```
Film what you see and your reaction to it, in one take.

DualShot records your iPhone's front and back cameras at the same time. It works on iPhone XS, XR and every iPhone since, not just the newest models.

TWO CAMERAS, TWO FILES
Every recording is saved as separate back-camera and front-camera videos, ready to edit, plus one combined video in the layout you chose. Pick which ones to keep in Export Options.

PICTURE IN PICTURE OR SPLIT
Drag the front-camera window to any corner, or split the screen top and bottom. The combined video follows your layout.

PHOTO MODE
Take a photo with both cameras at the same moment, in HEIF or JPEG, with flash and a self-timer.

ZOOM WHILE YOU RECORD
Move between your back lenses mid-take, from ultra wide to telephoto on Pro models. DualShot handles the lens switching for you.

MADE FOR YOUR IPHONE
On first launch DualShot measures what your iPhone can do and offers only the resolutions and frame rates that will work, up to 4K on newer models.

HEAT-UP SETTINGS
Running two cameras is hard work. DualShot watches your iPhone's temperature and lowers the frame rate before iOS has to stop recording. Prefer full quality? Turn it off.

FEELS LIKE THE CAMERA APP
Familiar controls, a swipe-up controls sheet, and a top row you can customise.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your videos and photos stay on your iPhone.

Great for vlogs, reactions, interviews, tutorials, travel, and capturing moments with the people behind the camera.

Requires iPhone XS, XR or later.
```

## App Review information

**Sign-in required:** No

**Notes for the reviewer**
```
DualShot records the front and back cameras at the same time using AVCaptureMultiCamSession, so it needs a physical iPhone with an A12 chip or later (iPhone XS / XR or newer); the Simulator has no cameras.

How to test:
1. Launch the app and allow Camera, Microphone and (when saving) Photos access. On first launch it spends a few seconds measuring which formats the iPhone can record with two cameras; this happens once.
2. Tap the red button to record, then tap again to stop. The back-camera video, front-camera video and a combined video are saved to Photos (configurable via the six-dot button > Export Options).
3. Switch to PHOTO in the mode switcher to take a photo with both cameras.
4. Drag the small front-camera window to move it; the six-dot button opens all controls.

No account or network connection is needed. The app collects no data.
```
